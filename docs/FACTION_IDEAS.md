# Faction ideas (the user's brainstorm, 2026-10-21)

These are ideas for later, not decisions. The current line-up: a normal, slightly boring Age of Empires faction (Meadowfolk), adorable froglings (Fenfolk), and nightmarish bugs (the Wildwelds).

## Next: a hyper-cute cartoon faction

- **The look:** over-the-top cute: rainbow unicorns, kittens.
- **The style:** bright and Dr. Seuss-like. Playful animations and body types. Crazy contraptions like Sylvester McMonkey McBean's Star-On machine, which puts stars on bellies. Seussian pipes that bulge, expand and contract, and wander, wonky and inconsistent.
- **A cartoon homeworld,** very cartoony itself.
- **Building-heavy, and rewarded for it:** the opposite of the bugs, which have no buildings. Maybe living buildings that grow crazier.
- **"Dr. Seuss plays Factorio":** assembling Rube Goldberg machines (a cartoon contraption of many moving parts) with conveyor belts. That would need intermediate resources and animated buildings. Does it make sense in an RTS? Hopefully; it could be very cool.

## The Nightmare Bugs

Keep developing them. They already took over a big piece of design space: no buildings at all. What other creative space should they explore?

## Why

If this is just Age of Empires in the browser, that already exists. Novel gameplay, interesting aesthetics and fresh strategy give people something to get excited about.

## Direction

The original concept was that anyone could vibecode their own faction on a rigorous engine. That feels restrictive now: balance is hard even with the physics, and nobody else has brought a faction. The user is leaning toward making about 8 cool factions and having the game be those.
