# New faction: notes

The user's decisions so far, as given. Nothing here is designed beyond what they said. Ask before adding names, flavor or specifics.

## How it works
- **The mother's refinement sets which eggs she can lay:** anything up to her own refinement. The game starts with an r1 mother laying T1 eggs. She mutates twice (+1.0 each, to r2 then r3), unlocking T2 and then T3 eggs.
- **A T2 larva can also become a mother** (the usual one step up, so r3): a paid shortcut to a top mother.
- **Principle:** the faction picks from the engine's options and adds no self-imposed handicaps. The natural rules do the gating.
- **A mother unit lays eggs.** There are eggs of different sizes. She trains them in her own body, like a building: a queue, each paid up front. She keeps moving while they grow.
- **Eggs and cocoons are shells.** A shell is a unit with its own statblock: its own body makeup, but the mass of what went in. It costs the same as the unit that went into it.
- **A laid egg** is born and starts changing into a larva straight away. It pays the larva's price less its own, once it has been Given enough.
- **Changing, step by step:** you have a larva and click its next form. The larva becomes a shell (an egg) at the same mass, instantly. The shell brews the next form, possibly growing, eating from its pack. When it's done, the new form emerges from it.
- **Food:** eggs sit, and change once they're Given enough food. Larvae eat food, and when they have enough, they can change.
- **Food modes:** both up front and eaten-as-it-goes are kept available.
- **Orders while brewing:** a shell takes orders; a new order cancels the brew and refunds the food it had paid.
- **Refinement:** each change can raise it by up to 1.0. Changing is how the faction climbs.
- **Animations:** very cool transformation animations for everything (entering a shell, emerging).

## Tiers
- **Three tiers of egg and larva:** refinement 1, 2 and 3, each an egg and a larva.

## What each tier's larva can become (offshoots, one refinement up). All confirmed.
- **T1:** the charger (a small body like the Vimmer's on two long, muscular legs, striding long), micropikes; and the T2 larva. The T1 larva itself can eat a tree (see Food source).
- **T2:** the Vimmer (the flier: an insectoid flying-V that dives and charges, stands on its wings, leaps up and over backward), ground-attack bodies, a slinger, the mother; and the T3 larva.
- **T3:** the Dragon Fly (flier) and ground-attack bodies.

## Dragon Fly (T3 flier)
- A fire-breathing dragon with butterfly wings, with insectoid and reptilian features.
- A few wing patterns, about 3.
- **Fire-breathing:** a conversion of crystal. The mechanic is TBD.
- **Feeding:** a larva fills its own pack, then needs food brought to it. The mother harvests and carries food to her eggs.
- **Food source:** the T1 larva can turn a whole tree into food (ratio 1, instant, with an animation). The tree becomes a semi-digested tree, and the larva is absorbed, giving no food of its own. (There's no separate Tree Worm.) The engine ability is `digestsTrees`.
- **Each larva can also go into a shell and become the next tier's larva:** T1 → T2 → T3.
- **Work refinement equals refinement** for the larvae (being tried).
- **Tier prices being tried:** 35 / 70 / 140 food, each double the last.

## Eggs
- **Whitish, origami-like structures,** sized by tier, and tinted slightly the colour of the larva they'll become (green, cyan, purple/pink).

## Larvae
- Like caterpillars, with size and colour set by refinement:
  - small, green;
  - medium, cyan;
  - big, purple/pink.

## Their world
- **Maybe a bug-native world:** the faction may get its own homeworld, the look of its half of the map (ground, trees, bushes, clutter, wildlife), like the Fenfolk's fen. For now it uses the default land.
