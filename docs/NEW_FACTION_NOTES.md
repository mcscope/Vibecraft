# New faction: notes

The user's decisions so far, as given. Nothing here is designed beyond what they said. Ask before adding names, flavor or specifics.

## How it works
- **The mother mutates twice herself.** Each later stage lets her lay bigger eggs.
- **A mother unit lays eggs.** There are eggs of different sizes. She trains them in her own body, like a building: a queue, each paid up front. She keeps moving while they grow.
- **Eggs and cocoons are shells.** A shell is a unit with its own statblock: its own body makeup, but the mass of what went in. It costs the same as the unit that went into it.
- **A laid egg** is born and starts changing into a larva straight away. It pays the larva's price less its own, once it has been Given enough.
- **Changing, step by step:** you have a larva and click its next form. The larva becomes a shell (an egg) at the same mass, instantly. The shell brews the next form, possibly growing, eating from its pack. When it's done, the new form emerges from it.
- **Food:** eggs sit, and change once they're Given enough food. Larvae eat food, and when they have enough, they can change.
- **Food modes:** both up front and eaten-as-it-goes are kept available.
- **Orders while brewing:** a shell takes orders; a new order cancels the brew and refunds the food it had paid.
- **Refinement:** each change can raise it by up to 1.0. Changing is how the faction climbs.
- **Animations:** very cool transformation animations for everything (entering a shell, emerging).

## Tiers
- **Three tiers of egg and larva:** refinement 1, 2 and 3, each an egg and a larva.

## What each tier's larva can become (offshoots, one refinement up)
- **T1:** a fast charging body. Maybe also micropikes.
- **T2:** flier bodies and ground-attack bodies, plus a slinger. Maybe also the mother (but that might leave T1 anemic).
- **T3 (to confirm; the user wrote "T2 and T2"):** flier bodies and ground-attack bodies.
- **Each larva can also go into a shell and become the next tier's larva:** T1 → T2 → T3.
- **Work refinement equals refinement** for the larvae (being tried).
- **Tier prices being tried:** 35 / 70 / 140 food, each double the last.

## Larvae
- Like caterpillars, with size and colour set by refinement:
  - small, green;
  - medium, cyan;
  - big, purple/pink.
